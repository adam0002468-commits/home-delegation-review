Our civic responsibility

Democracy works only when voters know who they're hiring. By using this page, I commit to:

  • Know everyone on my ballot, not just the names I recognize.
  • Follow the money: who funds each candidate, and who lobbies on the bills they touch.
  • Judge them by their votes, not their ads.
  • Watch for conflicts of interest, and stay skeptical but fair. Money and votes lining up isn't proof of a deal, but it's worth asking about.
  • Show up: call their office, go to town halls, and vote in every election, primaries included.

Everything here comes from public records, including the FEC, Congress, and Senate lobbying disclosures. We show only what's disclosed, say when data is missing, and never score anyone.
